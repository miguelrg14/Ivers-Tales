# Business plan

*Business Management course (UDIT, 4th year), IguanaGo group. English summary of the
group's business-plan spreadsheet. The spreadsheet was built on the course's business
plan simulator template, so some sections (sample competitors, legal and operations
boilerplate, dates) kept the template's example content; only the parts written for
IguanaGo and the financial projections are summarised here.*

## Company background

As the indie scene kept gaining success and visibility, the team wanted to make a game
for a broad, accessible audience. The first idea was a 2D platformer / metroidvania,
but the project quickly moved to 3D to match the team's ambitions and skills.

Because the IP and core idea are large, the team chose a focused, well-understood genre,
the dungeon crawler, taking strong inspiration from Nintendo: *The Legend of Zelda:
Link's Awakening* for design and mechanics and *The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker*
for soundtrack and sound. The game also aims to innovate with a different pace than
usual, new mechanics and puzzles with a different approach.

The project was conceived from the start to look for publishers and support, so the
financial and social-media plan was born together with the game as the basis for
founding and growing the company.

## Founding team

Alicia Díaz, Ariel Díaz, Iago López, Lucas García and Miguel Rodríguez, all students of
Video Game Development at UDIT with several free games released, plus Celia Rodríguez
(marketing).

## Positioning

> IguanaGo positions itself as a bastion of indie games, putting quality before
> quantity. We focus on few but outstanding games to consolidate our presence in the
> international market. Based in Spain with a global vision, our PC games, especially
> adventure games (dungeon crawlers), offer an immersive, story-rich experience for a
> demanding English-speaking audience. The company is built on respect,
> professionalism and internal organisation, and towards our players we promote
> inclusion, security and constant support. As a versatile and resilient team, we face
> the challenges of game development with precision and determination.
