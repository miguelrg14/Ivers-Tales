# Elevator pitch

*Translated from the pitch deck presented in the Business Management course (UDIT, 4th year).*

## The pitch

*Iver's Tales* is a proposal by IguanaGo! to develop a dungeon game for PC and
PlayStation. It combines magic and technology with the arcane and mysterious, in
gameplay that is unique but familiar and accessible.

We want to grow the dungeon-crawler genre. Few games have been made in recent years,
but the community values it because it brings back the experience of the classics. We
target an adult audience that can enjoy a mature, dark and powerful story.

The game is meant to be the foundation of a much larger universe. We hope you enjoy the demo.

## Synopsis

- **Description:** single-player 3D dungeon crawler for PC focused on exploration and
  combat, developed in Unity.
- **Story:** you control Iver, a small robot cat sent to retrieve a gem for her
  owner. During the adventure in the gothic world of Diance, Iver discovers what her
  mission really hides.
- **Trailer:** <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PyGUNLEBmyA>

## IP: Aeternal's Cycle

- A large universe built around a great secret.
- *Iver's Tales* is the first game of the universe, set in the flaming kingdom of Diance.
- Dramatic genre with a dark, mysterious tone. Iver faces the trials of EV and DAM to
  recover the gem for her mistress, Nemea.
- **Cast:** Iver, Nemea, EV, DAM.

## Target audience

- Players over 18
- English-speaking
- Explorer and achiever player types

## Gameplay mechanics

- **Basic attack:** a hammer attack to defend against the dungeon's enemies.
- **Dash:** a quick move to cross pits or dodge attacks.
- **Spells (the innovative mechanic):** unlocked at the start of each level and
  switchable at any time; each element has a different use:
  - **Light:** lights up the room to reveal hidden elements or dispel translucent ones.
  - **Fire:** ranged attack to defeat distant enemies or light torches that open doors.
  - **Air:** a gust of wind that moves large, heavy objects.

## Market and profitability

- Dungeon crawlers have many followers and many players who leave Steam reviews.
- Free-to-play titles are rare in the genre, so players are willing to pay for the
  game: revenue comes mainly from direct sales.
- It is one of the most profitable genres today, with few releases per year, which
  means little competition.
- Average earnings for the genre are above €10,000.

## Monetisation

Direct sales plus social-media marketing to make the IP visible for future games.

| Data | |
|---|---|
| Average revenue for the genre | €30,000 |
| Average price of similar games | €9.71 |
| New games of the same style since 2019 | 574 |
| Store fee (Steam, up to $10M) | 30% |
| Store fee (PlayStation) | 30% |
| Estimated production cost | ~€15,000 |
| **Final price** | **€9.99** |

Calculation: after the 30% store fee, each sale nets 0.7 × €9.99 ≈ €6.99, so covering
€15,000 of production costs requires about **2,145 sales**.

## Company: IguanaGo

A new Spanish indie PC game studio focused on stylised adventure games with a strong
story, with a versatile and resilient team. Our goal is to establish ourselves in the
international market with few but good games, growing the team, giving investors
confidence and reaching a global audience. Our internal work is based on respect,
professionalism and order, together with security and customer support.

## Promotion strategy

A strong link with players through social media (Instagram, X, TikTok), with polls,
content previews and transparent communication about development. Planned social
features: international leaderboards and a workshop for community maps. Exclusive
branded content was in development.

## Conclusions

- **The game** combines magic and technology, focuses on exploration, and lets players
  overcome each level by mastering the three elements.
- **Is it profitable?** After researching the competition and the expected return, we
  believe the game is profitable when combined with our marketing and localisation
  strategies.
- **The team** has an expert in each discipline plus external support, so we can
  deliver a quality project.
