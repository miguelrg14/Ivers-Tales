# Mission, vision and values

*Business Management course (UDIT, 4th year) – Miguel Rodríguez Gallego, IguanaGo group.
Translated from Spanish.*

## Mission

A new indie video game company focused on PC games, working from Spain for an
English-speaking audience. We make stylised adventure games (dungeon crawlers) with an
extra layer of immersive story, built by a versatile and resilient team.

## Vision

To become a company established in the international market that focuses on few but
good games, keeps improving its team, gives investors confidence and reaches a global
audience.

## Values

Our internal work is based on respect, professionalism and order, together with
inclusion, security and customer support.
