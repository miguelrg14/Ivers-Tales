# Production plan

*Iver's Tales* was produced by **IguanaGo Studios**, a team of seven students at UDIT
(Madrid) plus external support, between **September 2023 and May 2024**. Production was
run by Miguel Rodríguez (producer) with weekly check-ins, two-week sprints and a shared
task tracker that started in Notion and was moved to a spreadsheet in November 2023.

This document is an English summary of the original production spreadsheet
(*Plan de Producción*). Sheets that were only templates or superseded task lists are
not reproduced.

Weekly sprints were used until the first-term exam; from January 2024 sprints lasted
two weeks. Each sprint row in the tracker lists its tasks with owner, status
(completed / postponed / cancelled), estimate and actual hours.

## Milestones

- **Pre-production** – idea, game design document (GDD), art style guide, narrative premise.
- **Alpha** – first playable level, presentation, web page and feedback questionnaire.
- **Beta** – gameplay polish, feedback form, build and page published on
  [itch.io](https://iguanago.itch.io/ivers-tales).
- **Final presentations** – January 2024 presentations and the May 2024 delivery.
- **Showcases** – PS Talents and Match In Games 2024.

Scope was cut along the way: the third level, several boss animations, extra
worldbuilding, the Steam release and a board-game spin-off were cancelled to protect
the quality of the demo.

## Playtesting and feedback

Every week the team reviewed the current build ("weekly demo") and logged observations
and bugs (category, affected areas and how to reproduce them). Alpha and beta builds
were shared with players together with an online feedback questionnaire.
