# Publisher outreach

To look for funding and distribution, the team researched **61 publishers** and rated
each one by how well its catalogue fitted *Iver's Tales* (a 3D dungeon crawler). The
best matches were contacted with a personalised cover letter
([publisher-cover-letters.pdf](../business/publisher-cover-letters.pdf)) that presented
the studio, the game and the trailer.

## Publishers contacted

| Publisher | Notes from the research |
|---|---|
| Jandusoft | Many releases, some similar to ours (Aztec Tiki Talisman, LootLite) |
| Skystone Games | Same or similar genres |
| tinyBuild Games | Strong indie catalogue across genres |
| 2tainment GmbH | One game close to ours |
| 2Awesome Studio | Many games with a similar style and genre |
| Hidden Trap | Small publisher with similar titles (Bit Dungeon Plus) |
| Annapurna Games | – |
| Playtonic Friends | – |
| 10tons | – |
| Merge Games | Several interesting and similar titles |

## Discarded

Most of the remaining publishers were discarded because they focus on mobile, have no
games similar to ours, no longer publish external games or appeared to be inactive.
Larger publishers such as Devolver Digital and Team17 were noted as good fits but
likely to ask for terms the team could not meet at that stage.
